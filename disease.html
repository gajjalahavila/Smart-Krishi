🌿 Plant Health Guide

Identify common symptoms and learn ways to protect your crops.

🦠 Common Plant Problems

Learn about common crop symptoms and preventive practices.

🍂

Leaf Spot

Symptoms: Brown or dark spots appearing on leaves.

Prevention: Avoid excess moisture on foliage and monitor crops regularly.

🥀

Wilting

Symptoms: Leaves droop and plants lose their freshness.

Prevention: Check soil moisture, drainage and root health.

🟡

Yellow Leaves

Symptoms: Leaves turn yellow or lose their green color.

Prevention: Check water levels, nutrient balance and soil conditions.

🍄

Fungal Infection

Symptoms: Powdery growth or unusual patches on plant parts.

Prevention: Maintain spacing and avoid unnecessary water stagnation.

🐛

Pest Attack

Symptoms: Holes in leaves, damaged stems or visible insects.

Prevention: Inspect plants often and seek local agricultural advice.

🌱

Root Problems

Symptoms: Weak growth, poor plant development or root damage.

Prevention: Maintain proper drainage and avoid overwatering.

💡 Farmer's Reminder

Plant symptoms can have different causes. Observe the crop carefully and consult an agriculture expert before applying pesticides or treatments.
